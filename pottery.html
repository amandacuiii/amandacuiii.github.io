the first 100

I'm working toward creating 100 ceramic pieces before leaving Taiwan. Each piece marks a moment of learning, experimentation, and growth in my pottery practice, and many of them will be gifted to people who have shaped my time here.

Through this project, I've been experimenting with structure and the space between art and everyday use. I love how my creations can be both useful and expressive.

In June 2026, I'll be hosting an exhibit to share this collection: a record of my time in Taiwan, told through 100 pieces shaped by hand.
