Liminal

A Record of Creation In Between Stages
liminal  ˈlɪm·ɪ·nəl
Of or relating to a threshold; the space between what was and what has not yet become. Neither here nor there. Neither ending nor beginning.

I made these pieces during a year I knew was temporary. Liminality found me everywhere here. In the question of home; what it means to build one in a place you know you'll leave. In my identity, as a Cantonese American navigating belonging in a country that is itself navigating its own uncertain future. In pottery, a process that is nothing but thresholds: wet to leather-hard, raw to fired, formless to form.

To exist liminally is to resist resolution. To stay, for a little while longer, in the in-between.
