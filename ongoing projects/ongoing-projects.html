About the Camp

"Sa'atomo" (阿美陶部落營隊) means "come make pottery" in Amis. The camp is held in Guangfu, one of the cultural heartlands of the Amis, Taiwan's largest indigenous group. Clay is gathered by hand from the earth, shaped by pinching and coiling rather than thrown on a wheel, and fired in an open pit.

The camp emphasises process over product — each step carries its cultural context, from the land offering ritual to what the firing means. It is run with Austronesian Alien Studio, an indigenous-led studio founded by Papay, and taught alongside Amis artisans and elders.

What You'll Do
  • Soil gathering ritual (素材蒐集)
  • Dig and work the clay (挖土練土)
  • Hand-building & sculpting (陶器創作)
  • Wild pit firing + tool making (陶器野燒)
  • Plant workshop (植物工作坊)
  • Spirit workshop (靈觀工作坊)
  • Music & dance workshop (樂舞工作坊)
  • Tree bark art
Included

Not included: visa fees, personal travel insurance, taxis, shipping for finished pieces, and personal expenses. The camp is conducted primarily in Mandarin, with an English interpreter present. Open to English-speaking participants aged 18 and above.
