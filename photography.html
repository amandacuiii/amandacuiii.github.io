About
who I am

I'm Amanda Cui — a visual storyteller and Fulbright scholar documenting life, community, and craft across cultures. My work spans photography, documentary film, ceramics, and cultural education.

Based between New York and Taiwan, I create content that feels considered, intimate, and real. I'm drawn to projects that sit at the intersection of creativity and culture — brands that have a story worth telling.

Whether it's a short film, a photo series, or a branded reel, I bring the same eye for quiet detail and human connection to every collaboration.
