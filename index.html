Hi, I'm Amanda! Welcome to my corner of the internet.

I'm a visual storyteller interested in people, place, and the small moments that make everyday life feel meaningful. I work across photography, video, and ceramic as a way of paying attention and making sense of the world around me.

After graduating from the University of Pennsylvania with a degree in Finance and a minor in Fine Arts, I moved to Taiwan as a Fulbright English Teaching Fellow. Living abroad pushed me to create more intentionally, and lately I've been thinking about how to make creativity part of my life instead of something I save for later. This site is where I collect that process: projects, experiments, and in-between thoughts, as I figure out how to keep creating alongside everything else.
